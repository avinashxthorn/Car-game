## Saini Drive

Landscape 3D countryside driving with the **Made by Saini** studio intro.

Download **Saini-Drive.apk** below, open it on Android, and allow installation from your browser or Files app if asked.

- Offline green countryside with a winding road and traffic
- Touch wheel, arrow controls, or phone tilt; adjustable sensitivity
- Accelerator, brake, rechargeable nitro, and automatic acceleration option
- Animated 3D car, shadows, wheel rotation, suspension motion, and three camera views
- Free Drive and a 3 km timed sprint
- Four car colours, two lighting presets, engine audio, and vibration
- High / Smooth graphics settings

Android 8.0+, recent Android System WebView, and WebGL 2 support required. This is a development APK, signed with a debug key. Later builds may require uninstalling the previous build. Real-device performance depends on the phone; no universal frame-rate guarantee.
